# Surface: archive main (`dist/index.html`, gallery section)

Mode: Experience (portfolio showcase of an archive of Korean traditional buildings).
Audience: portfolio reviewers and visitors judging first impression and craft.
Constraints: keep the room's drafting sequence and 3D layer reading untouched; records will grow beyond 근정전; user-pinned form = horizontally arranged selectable items rendered as Joseon thread-bound books (고서, 선장본); clicking a book opens it and the visitor enters the book. (User switched from folding screens to books mid-build.)

## Direction contract
THESIS: The archive is a row of thread-bound books on hanji, one book per building; opening one is entering it. It refuses the literal hanok facade and the web card carousel.
OWN-WORLD: hanji ground, ink-black type, gardenia-yellow covers with an embossed 능화문 diamond pattern, five-hole red-thread binding (오침안정법) on the right edge, a white 제첨 title slip with vertical hanja title, page-block edges, vermilion only for seals.
STORY: Visitor sees one book forward with 勤政殿圖說 on its slip and a blank next book beside it, understands it is a growing shelf, turns the books, opens one; the spread shows the model-rendered ink drawing across the gutter; the camera dives into the page and the drafting room begins.
FIRST VIEWPORT: active book centred at ~46svh tall on a floor line at 41%, tilted 16° to show its fore-edge; next book receded beside it; caption bottom-left (hanja title, meta, summary, 入 seal button "책을 펼쳐 들어가기"); pager bottom-right "第一卷 / 전 1권"; mark 반닫이 top-left.
FORM: user-pinned book shelf; seed 43cb10db.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
